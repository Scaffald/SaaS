# Privacy Policy

*Last Updated: March 20, 2025*

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to Scaffald ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our platform.

Scaffald is a platform that connects construction organizations with workers, making it easier for organizations to hire and manage workers and teams. Workers can create profiles and receive a Scaffald score based on the information they provide, helping organizations quickly assess and select the best candidates for their projects.

By accessing or using Scaffald, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our platform.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Personal Information

We may collect the following types of personal information:

- **Account Information**: Name, email address, password (encrypted), profile picture, and other information you provide when creating an account.
- **Profile Information**:
  - For workers: Professional experience, skills, certifications, education, location, availability, and other information relevant to your professional profile.
  - For organizations: Company name, industry, size, location, and other business-related information.
- **Identity Verification**: Information used to verify your identity, which may include government-issued identification documents.
- **Payment Information**: Credit card details, bank account information, and billing addresses when you make payments through our platform. Note that payment information is processed by our third-party payment processor, Stripe.
- **Communications**: Information you provide when contacting us or communicating with other users through our platform.

### 2.2 Usage Data

We automatically collect certain information about how you interact with our platform:

- **Device Information**: IP address, browser type, operating system, device type, and other technical information about your device.
- **Log Data**: Information about your use of our platform, including pages visited, features used, actions taken, time spent, and other interaction data.
- **Location Data**: General location information derived from your IP address or more precise location if you grant us permission.
- **Cookies and Similar Technologies**: Information collected through cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies. See our Cookie Policy section for more details.

### 2.3 Content

- **Images and Media**: Photos, documents, or other media you upload to your profile or share through our platform.
- **User-Generated Content**: Reviews, ratings, messages, and other content you create or share on our platform.

- Enforce our Terms of Service and other policies

Your information may be stored and processed in any country where we have operations or where our service providers are located. By using Scaffald, you consent to the transfer of information to countries outside of your country of residence, which may have different data protection rules.

## 7. Cookie Policy

### 7.1 What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and provide information to the website owners.

### 7.2 Types of Cookies We Use

- **Strictly Necessary Cookies**: Essential for the operation of our platform. These cookies enable core functionality such as security, network management, and account access.
- **Functional Cookies**: Help us remember your preferences and settings to enhance your experience.
- **Analytics Cookies**: Collect information about how you use our platform, helping us improve its functionality and user experience.
- **Marketing Cookies**: Track your browsing habits to deliver advertising relevant to your interests.

### 7.3 Cookie Management

You can manage your cookie preferences through our cookie consent banner when you first visit our platform. You can also control cookies through your browser settings, which allow you to delete existing cookies and set preferences for future cookies.
